D&L Transport

M-J App Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

In short: M-J App is a paperwork-submission tool. It does not require an account, phone number, driver profile, location access, contacts, or advertising identifier. A user enters a Pro number, selects or photographs paperwork, and submits it to the office.

Information the app processes

When paperwork is submitted, the app processes the information needed to receive and manage that paperwork:

  • the Pro / Aljex number entered by the user;
  • the paperwork images selected or photographed by the user;
  • the time the submission is received; and
  • a randomly generated installation identifier used only to protect the submission service from excessive automated requests.

What the app does not require

M-J App does not require a user account, login, name, phone number, driver record, GPS location, contact list, or access to internal dispatch, accounting, rate, or analytics information. Paperwork can be submitted from any authorized user's phone regardless of who owns the device.

Camera and photo-library access

Camera access is used only when the user chooses to photograph paperwork. Photo-library access is used only when the user chooses existing paperwork images. The app does not scan or upload other photos automatically.

Local storage and failed submissions

To prevent paperwork from being lost when a connection fails, the app may keep an unfinished Pro number and prepared paperwork images in the app's private local storage. A successful submission clears that local draft. A saved retry also carries a random submission identifier so retrying the same paperwork can be recognized without creating a duplicate office record.

How submitted information is used

Submitted paperwork is used for D&L Transport / M-J operational purposes, including matching paperwork to loads, reviewing unmatched submissions, maintaining business records, and correcting paperwork attachments when needed. The mobile app does not contain advertising or behavioral-tracking features.

Storage and access

Submitted images are stored in private application storage and are intended to be accessible only through authorized internal business systems. The mobile app itself cannot browse other submissions or internal load data.

Rate-limit identifier

The installation identifier is randomly generated by the app and is not a phone number, username, advertising ID, or driver identity. The intake service hashes it for short-lived abuse-rate limiting. It is not used to match paperwork to a driver or load.

Retention

Submitted paperwork may be retained as an operational or business record according to company practices and applicable recordkeeping needs. Removing an item from an office inbox does not necessarily mean the underlying business record is immediately destroyed.

Security

The mobile app submits paperwork through a dedicated intake service. It does not receive direct database or storage credentials and does not provide public access to internal D&L Transport / M-J operational data.

Questions

For questions about a paperwork submission or this privacy policy, contact the D&L Transport office or your normal dispatch contact.

This policy applies to the M-J App mobile paperwork-submission application.
