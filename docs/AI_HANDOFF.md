## Driver paperwork architecture — current decision

M-J-App is a standalone Expo/React Native app; field users do not use M-J-Site.

There is intentionally **no sender verification or identity requirement**:

1. Open app.
2. Enter Aljex/Pro number.
3. Take/select paperwork images.
4. Submit.

No username, password, phone OTP, sender phone, driver lookup, or account is required. The submitting person may be the driver, dispatcher, a friend, or someone using a second phone. The system only cares about the entered Pro number and paperwork images.

A random non-personal install ID is generated by the app and sent in `X-MJ-Install-ID` solely for rate limiting. It is not an account, not business identity, not shown to staff, and is not stored on `paperwork_submissions`.

## Matching

- Pro/Aljex numbers are not unique; ambiguous matches must never be guessed.
- Exactly one reliable live match attaches automatically.
- No match or multiple matches are accepted and shown to staff as Needs Review.
- Archived unique matches are preserved for staff handling.
- The public app always receives only `Paperwork received.` after durable acceptance; internal matching status is not exposed to the field user.

## Public-intake safeguards

The Edge Function is the only public write boundary. It enforces:

- POST only
- `X-MJ-Install-ID` format validation
- SHA-256 hashed, short-lived per-install throttling: 20 submissions / 10 minutes
- global safety valve: 2000 submissions / 10 minutes
- 1–12 images
- 15 MB maximum per image
- 60 MB maximum total submission
- image MIME allow-list: jpeg/png/webp/heic/heif
- numeric Pro validation
- server-side load matching
- no operational/load details in the response

Security verification completed after deployment:

- `paperwork_intake_rate_limits` has RLS enabled and intentionally has no client policies.
- anon/authenticated clients cannot read or write the rate-limit table and cannot execute its RPC.
- `service_role` alone can execute `consume_paperwork_rate_limit`.
- anon has no direct SELECT/INSERT table grants on `paperwork_submissions` or `paperwork_images`.
- internal authenticated staff retain explicit grants plus role-gated RLS.
- private Storage remains accessible to internal roles only.

- staff assign/delete RPCs remain `SECURITY INVOKER`.
