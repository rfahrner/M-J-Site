Historical Load Archive

Admin/IT only. Export records to your computer, then upload the folder to OneDrive in your browser. No Microsoft connection to this site is needed.
Exporting never deletes anything. It copies records and images out of Supabase into the folder you choose and leaves the originals exactly where they are. Purging is a separate, later step you have to ask for, after you have confirmed the copies are intact — and analytics history is preserved before anything is removed. No Microsoft or OneDrive account is needed for any of this.

Export to your computer

  1. Choose the cutoff date below and refresh the preview. Only loads before that date are included.
  2. Click Choose Local Folder & Export and select a regular folder, such as Documents / Dispatch Archive.
  3. After export completes, open OneDrive in your browser, sign in to your own account, and upload the generated Archive folder.
  4. Wait for the upload to finish and open the uploaded files to verify them before using the optional purge.

Use desktop Chrome or Edge for folder export. Keep this tab open until export finishes. This site cannot verify your manual OneDrive upload.

Everything dated before this is included. Defaults to six months ago — change it and the counts below update.
